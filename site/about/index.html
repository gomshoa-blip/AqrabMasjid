  • العامل والمقيم: بلغته، بست لغات.
  • كبير السن: أزرار كبيرة وكلام بسيط.
  • الجميع: مجاني بالكامل، بدون إعلانات، وبدون حساب.

Aqrab Masjid — your guide to the houses of Allah, wherever you are

The idea. Many of us are on the road, in a new area, or in another city when prayer time comes. The same question comes up every time: where is the nearest mosque, and how long until iqama? Aqrab Masjid answers it in one tap: it shows the next prayer with adhan and iqama times, and takes you to the nearest mosque or Jami.

The goal. To make praying on time and in congregation easier for everyone: drivers and travellers (big clear screen, Android Auto), workers and residents (six languages), and older people (big buttons, simple words). Completely free, no ads, no account.

What it offers.

  • Nearest mosque and Jami, with directions.
  • Prayer and iqama times (Umm Al-Qura) with a countdown.
  • Adhan and iqama alerts, with your choice of sound.
  • In the car: next prayer and nearby mosques on the car screen.
  • Qibla with the camera.
  • Holy Quran (King Fahd Complex script) and daily adhkar.
  • Home screen widgets, and Qirat: funeral prayer mosques and cemeteries in Riyadh.

How you can help. Add a missing mosque with "I am at the mosque", share the app, and send us your ideas or any errors.

Our principles. Always free, no ads, no account, and we do not sell any data (privacy policy). Mosques we add go to OpenStreetMap, an open map for everyone.
